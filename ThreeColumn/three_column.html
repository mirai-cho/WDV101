Feeding

  • Wet food vs dry food: mix both to keep hydration and dental health.
  • Portion size: adjust based on weight and activity level.
  • Always provide fresh water in a clean bowl.

Grooming

Brush your cat a few times a week to reduce shedding and hairballs. Use nail clippers made for cats and trim only the clear tip of the claw. Regular grooming helps you check for skin problems or parasites early.

Play & Enrichment

Daily playtime with feather wands or toy mice keeps cats active and happy. Provide scratching posts, climbing shelves, and hiding spots. Rotate toys so your cat doesn’t get bored.
