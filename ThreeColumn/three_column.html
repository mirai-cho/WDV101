Feeding

Mix wet and dry food to support both hydration and dental health. Adjust portion sizes based on your cat's age, weight, and activity level.

Grooming

Regular brushing reduces shedding and hairballs. Use cat safe nail clippers and check the skin for any irritation or parasites.
