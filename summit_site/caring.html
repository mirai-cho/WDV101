How to take Alaskan Dogs

Training

Alaskan breeds are intelligent but can be strong-willed. They respond best to positive reinforcement, consistent routines and plenty of mental stimulation. Early socialization and structured training help them become confident and well-behaved companions.

Grooming

These dogs have thick double coats that shed heavily, especially during seasonal changes. Regular brushing helps reduce loose fur and keeps their coat healthy. Occasional baths and proper coat care are important to maintain cleanliness and prevent matting.

Health

Like many larger northern breeds, they can be prone to certain conditions such as hip dysplasia and eye disorders. Regular vet checkups, proper exercise and monitoring their weight all contribute to maintaining long-term health.

Nutrition

Alaskan breeds are active and burn a lot of energy. They benefit from high-quality, high-protein food that supports strong muscles and endurance. Adjusting their calorie intake based on activity level helps keep them at a healthy weight.
