Welcome to Summit Farms Rescue

Summit Farms is a rescue dedicated to saving Alaskan dog breeds such as Malamutes, Huskies and other northern working dogs. Since 2004, founders Brad and Janet Harker have helped more than 2,620 dogs find loving homes. Our mission is to reduce homelessness, improve animal welfare and educate our community about the challenges faced by these unique breeds. We are grateful for the support of our donors and invite you to learn more or contact us anytime.

Meet Our Dogs

We regularly welcome Alaskan breeds into our care, including Huskies and Malamutes. Even when no dogs are currently available, you can learn how our adoption process works and see dog's profiles on our Adoption page.
